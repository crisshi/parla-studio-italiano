City Libraries Are Becoming
Community Hubs

Leggi una notizia breve in inglese, poi consolida il nuovo vocabolario con spiegazioni ed esercizi in italiano.

Tempo di lettura: 3 min

For many people, a library is simply a quiet place to borrow books. However, libraries in several cities are changing. They are becoming community hubs where people can learn new skills, meet their neighbours, and find support.

One library in Manchester recently opened a small workshop with computers and craft tools. Visitors can use the space to create a CV, repair a bicycle, or learn how to use digital services. The project has been especially helpful for people who do not have these tools at home.

“We want everyone to feel welcome here,” said the library manager. “A modern library should be a place where people can connect, not just collect books.”

Similar projects are appearing across the UK. Experts say that these new services can make a real difference in local communities.
